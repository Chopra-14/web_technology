ADITYA UNIVERSITY

About: Located in Surampalem, Andhra Pradesh, Aditya Engineering College is a premier technical institution known for its sprawling eco-friendly campus.
It offers a wide range of NBA-accredited undergraduate and postgraduate programs in engineering, technology, and management.
The college delivers industry-oriented education, strong placement opportunities, and has recently transitioned under the umbrella of Aditya University.
